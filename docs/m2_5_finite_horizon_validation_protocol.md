# Frozen M2.5 finite-horizon validation protocol

Freeze date: 2026-09-15. This is M2.5, not Level 2 V3. It cannot revise V1 or V2.

## SOURCE

Cesa-Bianchi and Lugosi (2006), *Prediction, Learning, and Games*, Theorem 2.2, finite-time exponentially weighted average forecaster bound; auction instantiation follows the repeated-auction MWU environment used by Kolumbus and Nisan (2022) and Aggarwal et al. (2024).

## EXACT RESULT

For K actions, reward vectors in [0,1]^K, uniform initial weights, and p_t(a) proportional to exp(eta times cumulative reward through t-1), expected external regret satisfies

    E[R_T] <= log(K)/eta + eta*T/8.

With eta=sqrt(8 log(K)/T), the right side equals sqrt(T log(K)/2). The exact expected reward is computable prospectively because the opponent sequence is deterministic.

## MODEL

One learning bidder faces one deterministic scripted bidder in a repeated single-item auction. The learner's value is 1.0 and action grid is {0,.01,...,1}. The scripted rival's bid at round t is generated by the outcome-independent cycle [0.10,0.35,0.60,0.85,0.45,0.70,0.20,0.95], each held for 37 rounds. Ties use their exact expected allocation in the full-information vector. The same sequence is used at every horizon.

## LEARNER

Hedge/MWU with uniform weights and horizon-specific fixed eta=sqrt(8 log(101)/T). The action is sampled from the current policy; the complete mechanism-generated counterfactual reward vector is then observed.

## MECHANISM

First-price auction and second-price auction, no reserve, uniform tie-breaking. These are two preregistered strata of one learner-bound target.

## VALUATIONS

Learner value 1.0. Rival value 1.0 (its bid is scripted and never exceeds value). Thus every learner counterfactual utility is in [0,1].

## HORIZON GRID

10,000; 25,000; 50,000; 100,000; 200,000.

## SEEDS

Thirty independent learner seeds per mechanism and horizon. In addition, exact expected regret is computed deterministically by policy propagation; it is the primary test and is not subject to Monte Carlo error.

## PRIMARY METRIC

For every mechanism-horizon stratum: exact expected external regret divided by B(T,K,eta). The realized-regret mean and its 95% t confidence interval are a sampling audit.

## EXPECTED SCALING / QUANTITATIVE TARGET

The source inequality itself: exact expected regret <= B(T,K,eta)=sqrt(T log(101)/2). Normalized expected regret must not exceed one. This is a direct theoretical inequality, not a project-created cutoff.

## PASS CRITERION

PASS if the exact expected regret satisfies the source inequality in every one of the ten preregistered mechanism-horizon strata, all generated reward-vector entries are in [0,1], and an independent direct calculation agrees with the simulator counterfactual reward routine in tests.

## PARTIAL CRITERION

PARTIAL if the source inequality holds in at least one complete mechanism stratum but an implementation-consistency check or the other mechanism stratum fails, without evidence of a common learner defect.

## FAIL CRITERION

FAIL if any exact expected-regret inequality violation is confirmed after arithmetic verification, any reward leaves [0,1], or a common learner/counterfactual implementation inconsistency is found.

Monte Carlo confidence intervals are reported but cannot override the exact primary test.
