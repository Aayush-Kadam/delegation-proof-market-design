# Internal referee B - algorithmic game theory

## Major concerns

1. “Cross-learner robustness” could conflate positive signs with stable magnitudes.
2. Low regret is not shown to imply outcome robustness.
3. FTPL and regret matching are computationally tested but not covered by Hedge theory.
4. Reproduction needs tiered commands rather than a demand to rerun hundreds of millions of rounds.

## Minor concerns

- Define the exact full-information variants and random-state pairing.
- Report deterministic run IDs and completeness without narrating operational incidents.
- Separate policy-stability lemmas from the direct interval proof.

## Required revisions completed

- RM’s 0/3 magnitude falsification result is stated in the abstract-adjacent evidence section.
- Theory and computation are labeled separately.
- The manuscript explains why external regret is an algorithmic diagnostic rather than an economic guarantee.
- Reproduction is divided into quick, core-paper, and full-campaign tiers.

## Optional revisions

A later paper could study bandit feedback or additional algorithms, but those experiments are outside this frozen project.
