# M8 targeted novelty audit

General softmax/MWU sensitivity is known; the softmax Lipschitz step is not claimed as new. Growing-expert and discretization literatures also study action-set changes, but typically for regret rather than coupled endogenous auction trajectories.

The possibly distinct auction result is the explicit `4x3`, six-horizon rectangle with opposing FPA/SPA revenue and allocation rankings. The endogenous lemma is a direct synthesis of known softmax stability, bounded-game payoff Lipschitzness, and discrete Gronwall; its auction coupling formulation is useful but mathematically incremental.

Verdict: general mathematics **KNOWN/INCREMENTAL**; auction application **POSSIBLY DISTINCT**.
