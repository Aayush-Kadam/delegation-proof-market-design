# M6 theorem novelty audit

TH-M6-003 is an exact finite-horizon result for deterministic full-information Hedge: a closed three-parameter rectangle supports opposite FPA/SPA rankings on revenue and allocation for every horizon 2 through 9.

Kolumbus–Nisan study regret-minimizing agents and asymptotic/convergent FPA and SPA behavior. Aggarwal et al. study truthful-auction shading, learning-rate effects, and auctioneer regret. Feng et al. establish convergence for mean-based learners in a stochastic-value/exploration setting. Hoy–Jain–Wilkens give revenue-only guarantees under behavioral axioms. Smoothness work gives welfare bounds, and Ipsen–Skoulakis give FPA-only revenue rates under approximate CE/no-swap regret. Targeted searches found no matching consecutive finite-horizon, explicit-rectangle, simultaneous revenue/allocation reversal theorem.

Verdict: **POSSIBLY DISTINCT**, not a strong gap. The theorem remains tied to a small action model and does not transfer to the Level-3 full grid.
