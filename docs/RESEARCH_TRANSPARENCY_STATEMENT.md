# Research transparency statement

The project separates development, falsification, and holdout evidence prospectively. Protocols, candidate statements, environments, seeds, mechanisms, learners, economic thresholds, and verdict rules were frozen in version control before the corresponding powered outcomes were analyzed. Powered runners use deterministic identities, atomic persistence, and resumable validation. Negative results—including regret-matching magnitude weakness, heterogeneous revenue reversals, two failed mechanism families, theorem counterexamples, and an unopened mechanism holdout—remain in the public record. Code, compact processed evidence, certificates, checksums, and reproduction instructions accompany the manuscript. Large raw campaigns are archived separately where storage permits. The repository documents internal clean-room reproduction but does not claim independent external replication or proof review.
