# Delegation-Proof Market Design v1.1.1

Author: **Aayush Kadam**

This release asks how standard auction rankings change when bidders delegate repeated decisions to adaptive algorithms. Frozen finite-horizon evidence finds that first-price auctions tend to protect seller revenue while second-price auctions tend to protect allocation and welfare. Exact Hedge theory proves the conflict on explicit finite-action regions. Homogeneous Hedge, FTPL, and regret-matching evidence preserves the broad pattern; ordered heterogeneous profiles expose material learner-role effects and 29 revenue reversals with no allocation reversals.

Two prospective mechanism repairs fail unchanged development requirements. A scoped constant-hybrid certificate proves opposed revenue and allocation derivatives and incompatible safety requirements at `(v, eta_H, eta_L, H)=(1/3,4,0,9)`. No successful delegation-proof mechanism or universal impossibility is claimed.

The release contains 66 passing tests, compact processed evidence, deterministic theorem certificates, frozen protocols, tiered reproduction instructions, publication figures, the public paper, an anonymous journal-review PDF, and release archives. Internal clean-room reproduction passed; independent external replication remains pending.
