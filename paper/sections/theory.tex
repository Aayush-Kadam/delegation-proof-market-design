\subsection{A finite-horizon delegation conflict}
Consider values $(1,v)$, bid sets $\{0,v,1\}$ and $\{0,v\}$, fair ties, uniform initialization, and full-information Hedge. Let $x_t=\log(w_{H,t}(1)/w_{H,t}(v))$, $y_t=\log(w_{H,t}(v)/w_{H,t}(0))$, and $z_t=\log(w_{L,t}(v)/w_{L,t}(0))$. These ratios generate an exact three-dimensional recurrence (Appendix M6).

\paragraph{Restricted finite-horizon theorem.} For $v\in[.30,.36]$, $\eta_H\in[.8,1.2]$, $\eta_L\in[.05,.15]$, and every integer $H\in\{2,\ldots,9\}$, average FPA revenue strictly exceeds average SPA revenue, while SPA allocation efficiency strictly exceeds FPA allocation efficiency. Hence SPA welfare is also higher. The proof uses outward-rounded interval propagation over an exhaustive partition of the closed parameter rectangle.

This theorem isolates the mechanism behind the Level-3 sign conflict but does not establish the full $.01$-grid surface or its long-horizon quantitative magnitudes.
